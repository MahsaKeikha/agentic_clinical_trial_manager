# F52 L3 Gold Standard Audit

Promotion requires evidence on the exact merge head for:

- six substantive clinical-trial operations agents
- explicit fail-closed protocol, ethics, consent, eligibility, privacy, data-integrity, adverse-event, deviation, training, accountability, monitoring, and safety-review gates
- human approval that cannot override blockers
- expanded unit/integration/red-team tests
- eight-scenario held-out clinical-trial safety suite at 100% expected-behavior pass rate
- clean minimal and complete examples plus smoke execution
- Python 3.10, 3.11, and 3.12 CI green
- held-out artifact publication
- version 1.0.0, licensing/citation metadata, and reproducibility/safety documentation

L3 denotes reference-quality engineering maturity. It is not clinical validation, GCP certification, IRB approval, sponsor authorization, or regulatory clearance.
